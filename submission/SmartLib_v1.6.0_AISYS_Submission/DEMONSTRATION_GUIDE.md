## 10–15 Minute Evaluator Walkthrough Summary

Evaluators can follow this 12-stage sequential workflow using the pre-seeded evaluation accounts:

1. **Landing Page** (`/`): Browse institutional hero and public pathways.
2. **Staff Login** (`/login`): Click **Admin Demo** (`admin@smartlib.ac.in` / `Admin@123456`).
3. **Executive Dashboard** (`/`): Inspect 8 live KPI cards, weekly circulation charts, and category breakdown.
4. **Book Catalog** (`/books`): Search monographs and expand the physical copy drawer.
5. **Cataloguing** (`/cataloguing`): Add a new title with ISO 2108 dynamic ISBN and sequential accessions.
6. **Members Management** (`/members`): Register an institutional patron and bind contactless Smart Card UID.
7. **Circulation Desk** (`/circulation`): Checkout circulating copy (disarms EAS), return copy (re-arms EAS), and inspect fines.
8. **RFID Tagging & Handheld Inventory** (`/rfid/tagging`, `/inventory`): Encode EPC tags and run bulk shelf sweeps.
9. **Security Gate Monitoring** (`/security-gate`): Simulate unauthorized transit, fire EAS alarm, and inspect synthetic CCTV snapshot.
10. **Reports & Notifications** (`/reports`, `/notifications`): Export PDF/CSV reports and inspect notification queue.
11. **Public OPAC & Bookshelf** (`/opac`): Search unauthenticated catalog and manage private Virtual Bookshelf.
12. **Audit Trail & System Health** (`/audit`, `/backup`): Inspect immutable compliance logs and verify SQL backup restore.
